Projects

  • 2026 - present
    RDGSplat: Render-Dedicated Geometry for Novel View Synthesis

    UC Davis. Zheng et al., arXiv preprint, 2026.

    • Developed a parallel decoder branch predicting additional 3D Gaussians for novel-view rendering on a frozen 1.4B 3D foundation model, with a target-pose-conditioned adapter and photometric-only supervision.
    • Preserved depth and pose predictions, unlike backbone fine-tuning, with only 205M (14.7%) trainable parameters.
    • Improved RE10K PSNR from 20.9 to 25.1 dB and zero-shot PSNR by 2.7–3.8 dB on ACID, DL3DV, and DTU, outperforming pixelSplat and MVSplat on RE10K and DTU; gains held across three backbones.
  • 2026
    PRISM: Feed-Forward Single-Image 3D Reconstruction via Geometric Warp-Residual Modeling

    UC Davis. Zheng et al., arXiv preprint, 2026.

    • Built a feed-forward single-image 3D reconstruction pipeline that forward-warps input latents to target views using monocular depth, with a 26M transformer predicting residuals for LYRA’s frozen 3DGS decoder.
    • Eliminated diffusion sampling at inference, the main bottleneck of diffusion-based methods; geometric warping covers up to 90% of the target view, so diffusion supervision is needed only for training on 401 synthetic scenes.
    • Cut per-scene inference time by 277× over diffusion-based LYRA (2.8 h to 36 s) while staying within 0.8 dB of its PSNR on RE10K and DL3DV; achieved zero-shot generalization to three real-world benchmarks.
  • 2025 - 2026
    TTSA3R: Training-Free Temporal-Spatial Adaptive Persistent State for Streaming 3D Reconstruction

    UC Davis. Zheng et al., WACV 2027 LVR Workshop (Oral).

    • Proposed a training-free memory update rule for streaming 3D reconstruction that combines temporal state changes and observation–state alignment to compute token-wise update weights.
    • Mitigated catastrophic forgetting in long sequences caused by uniform memory updates, without additional training or parameters and while maintaining the base model’s speed (18 FPS).
    • Reduced ATE by 43% vs. CUT3R on TUM-dynamics, limited reconstruction error growth from 50 to 250 frames to 1.33× vs. 3.8×, and achieved the lowest pose error on 1,000-frame sequences.
  • 2022 - 2023
    LiDAR 3D Object Detection for Autonomous Driving

    Sun Yat-sen University. 4 journal publications, including 2 first-author papers (IEEE TMM, IEEE SPL).

    • Designed a bidirectional attention module to fuse raw-point and range-view features, providing dense range-view context for sparse points and improving Waymo mAP by 4.2 points over IA-SSD (IEEE TMM).
    • Proposed a dual-channel PointNet++/Transformer backbone with density-aware center features for local and long-range geometry, improving KITTI cyclist AP by 4.18 over IA-SSD with ~4× fewer parameters than PDV (IEEE SPL).
